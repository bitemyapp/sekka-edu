title: Pon's Lantern
level: 3
skills: fluency, expression
cover: tanuki, #b06a2e
about: Pon the tanuki wants to light the lantern path, but the wind keeps blowing out his candle.

On the night of the summer festival, there were ten paper lanterns along the path through the woods. It was Pon's job to light them.
===
Pon held his candle up to the first lantern. Whoosh! A gust of wind blew the flame right out.

"Oh no," said Pon. He lit the candle again and tried the second lantern. Whoosh! Out it went.
===
Pon sat down on a stump and sighed. "The festival starts soon," he said, "and every lantern is still dark."
===
Kiko the rabbit hopped out of the ferns. "Why so sad, Pon?"

"The wind keeps blowing out my candle," said Pon.

Kiko thought for a moment. Then she stood beside him with her long ears up, like a wall against the wind.
===
Pon lit the candle, and behind Kiko's ears, the little flame stayed tall. One lantern glowed orange. Then two. Then three.

Soon a fox and a frog came to help too, making a circle around the flame as Pon walked from lantern to lantern.
===
Four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten! The whole path shone like a string of tiny moons.

"I couldn't have done it alone," said Pon.

"That's what friends are for," said Kiko. And together they walked down the bright path to the festival.
