title: The Green Seed
level: 2
skills: vowel-teams, fluency
cover: leaf, #4f9b3a
about: Jay plants a seed and waits. What will it be?

Jay has a seed. It is a little green seed.
===
Jay digs a hole and plants the seed. "Grow, seed, grow," he says.
===
Each day, Jay gives the seed a drink of rain from a pail.
===
The sun shines. The rain falls. Jay waits and waits.
===
One day, a green sprout peeks up out of the soil.
===
The sprout grows and grows. It gets leaves. It gets a bud.
===
Then one morning, the bud opens. It is a big yellow flower!
===
"Hooray!" said Jay. "My seed is a flower. I need more seeds!"
