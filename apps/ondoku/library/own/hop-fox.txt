title: Hop, Fox!
level: 1
skills: short-o, sight-words, fluency
cover: fox, #d9772b
about: Fox finds a pot and thinks it is a hat. Is it?

Fox can hop.
===
Hop, hop, hop! Fox hops to a log.
===
Fox sits on top of the log.
===
Fox has a pot and a mop.
===
"Is it a hat?" said Fox.
===
Fox has the pot on top. It is not a hat!
===
Fox can not see. Bonk!
===
"It is a pot," said Fox. "A pot is not a hat."
===
Fox and the pot hop off.
