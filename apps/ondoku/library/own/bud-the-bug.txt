about: Bud is a little bug. A big duck is in the pond!

Bud is a bug. Bud has six legs.
===
Bud can zig and zag and zip.
===
Bud zips up a stem. Bud sits on top.
===
Bud can see the pond.
===
A big duck is in the pond!
===
The duck can see Bud.
===
Bud runs and hops in a gap.
===
The duck can not get Bud. Bud is snug in the gap.
===
"Yes!" said Bud.
