title: Fumi's Night Riddles
level: 3
skills: expression, vocabulary, fluency
cover: owl, #4b3a8c
about: Fumi the owl has riddles for you. Read each one, guess, then turn the page!

Hoo! I'm Fumi. I'm awake while you're asleep, so I think up riddles. Read each one out loud, and guess before you turn the page.
===
I have a face but no eyes. I have hands but no fingers. I tell you when it's time for bed. What am I?
===
A clock!
===
I fall from the sky but never get hurt. I'm cold and white, and no two of me are just the same. What am I?
===
A snowflake!
===
The more of me you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?
===
Footsteps!
===
I'm full of holes, but I can still hold water. What am I?
===
A sponge!
===
I come out at night without being called, and I'm gone every morning without being taken. What am I?
===
A star! Hoo, hoo! Now you know all my riddles. Try them on someone at home.
