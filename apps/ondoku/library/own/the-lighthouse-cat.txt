title: The Lighthouse Cat
level: 4
skills: vocabulary, fluency, expression
cover: cat, #2f5d7c
about: When fog rolls over Gull Rock, Tomas has to find his grandmother's cat before the storm arrives.

Gull Rock was a speck of granite in a restless sea, and on top of it stood a white lighthouse with a red door. Tomas spent every summer there with his grandmother, who had kept the light for thirty years, and with her cat, a sleek grey hunter named Mackerel.
===
Each evening, Tomas followed his grandmother up the spiral staircase, all one hundred and twelve steps of it, to the lantern room at the top. There she polished the great glass lens until it gleamed, and when the sun slipped below the horizon, she lit the lamp. Its beam swept across the water, warning ships away from the treacherous rocks below.
===
One afternoon, a wall of fog rolled in from the open sea, so thick that Tomas could barely see his own hands. "A gale is coming behind it," said his grandmother, frowning at the barometer. "Bring Mackerel inside, would you? He's out hunting crabs again."

Tomas searched the kitchen garden and the woodshed. He called and called, but the fog swallowed his voice. Mackerel was nowhere.
===
Then Tomas had an idea. Every evening at supper, his grandmother tapped a spoon against Mackerel's tin bowl, and the cat came running from wherever he was. Tomas fetched the bowl and the spoon and walked slowly along the edge of the rocks, tapping as he went. Ting, ting, ting.

For a long moment, there was only the slap of the waves. Then, out of the grey, came a small, offended meow.
===
Mackerel was crouched on a ledge just above the tide line, his fur beaded with mist and the water creeping higher with every wave. Tomas climbed down carefully, scooped him up, and hurried back to the red door just as the first fat raindrops began to fall.

That night the gale howled around the tower, but inside it was warm. The lamp turned steadily above them, Mackerel purred on the windowsill, and somewhere out in the dark, a ship saw the light and steered safely past Gull Rock.
