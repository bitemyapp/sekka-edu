title: Bear's Bike Ride
level: 2
skills: magic-e, fluency, expression
cover: bear, #8a5a3c
about: Bear rides his bike to the lake, and Mole has a kite.

Bear has a bike. It is red and white.
===
"I like to ride my bike," said Bear. "I will ride to the lake."
===
Bear rides up the hill. It is a long ride. Bear has to take a rest.
===
Bear rides down the hill. Whee! The wind is on his face.
===
At the lake, Bear sees Mole. Mole has a kite.
===
"Can I ride home with you?" said Mole. "Yes," said Bear. "Hop on!"
===
Bear and Mole ride home. Mole holds the kite, and it flies up in the wind!
===
"What a fine ride," said Bear. "What a fine kite," said Mole.
