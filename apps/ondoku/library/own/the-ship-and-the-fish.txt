title: The Ship and the Fish
level: 2
skills: digraphs, fluency, expression
cover: bell, #3f6aab
about: Chip and Beth are on a ship when something big swims past.

Chip and Beth sit on a ship. The ship has a bell. Ding, ding, ding!
===
"What is that?" said Chip. "Is it a fish?"
===
It is a big fish with a long tail. It can swish and splash.
===
The fish jumps up. Then it dips back down with a big splash!
===
"Look at that fish!" said Beth. "I wish I had a fish."
===
"That fish is not a pet," said Chip. "It must swim in the sea."
===
Chip and Beth wave to the fish. The fish swims off. Swish, swish!
