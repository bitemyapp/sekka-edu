Ten red hens sit in a pen.

The hens peck and peck.
===
Meg has a pet hen. The pet hen is Red.
===
Red hops up on the pen.
===
"Get back, Red!" said Meg.
===
Red sits on a nest. Red will not get up.
===
What is in the nest?
===
Ten eggs! Red is a mom.
