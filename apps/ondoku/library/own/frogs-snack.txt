title: Frog's Snack
level: 2
skills: blends, fluency, expression
cover: frog, #2f8f5b
about: Frog is hungry, but every bug is too quick for him. Then he tries a trick.

Frog sat on a flat rock in the pond. His tummy went grumble, grumble.
===
"I must get a snack," said Frog.
===
A fly went past. Frog jumped. Splash! The fly was too quick.
===
A black bug went past. Frog jumped. Splash! The bug was too quick.
===
Frog swam back to his rock. He did not jump. He did not blink. He sat still.
===
A moth flew past. Frog did not jump. Snap! Frog got his snack.
===
"Still is the best trick," said Frog. And then he had a nap in the sun.
