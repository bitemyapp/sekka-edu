title: The Star Garden
level: 3
skills: r-controlled, fluency, expression
cover: star, #3d4fa8
about: Mira wants to grow stars in her garden. Her grandpa has an idea.

Mira loved the stars. Every night she sat on the porch with Grandpa and counted them until her eyes grew heavy.
===
"I wish I could grow stars," she said one night. "I would plant a whole garden of them, right by the barn."

"Stars are too far away to plant," said Grandpa. Then he scratched his beard and smiled. "But maybe we can try something else."
===
The next morning, Grandpa handed Mira a small paper packet. Inside were seeds, thin and dark as burnt toast.

"These are moonflower seeds," he said. "Plant them by the fence, where the vines can climb."
===
Mira dug the dirt with her fingers. She pushed in the seeds, one by one, and watered them every morning before school.
===
For weeks, nothing happened. Then curly green vines crept up the fence, higher and higher, with big leaves shaped like hearts.

"Where are the stars?" asked Mira. Grandpa winked. "Wait until dark."
===
That evening, as the first star came out, the buds on the vines began to turn. Slowly, slowly, they opened into wide white flowers, round and bright as the moon.
===
Mira gasped. "It's a star garden!"

"And it only blooms at night," said Grandpa, "just like the real one."

They sat on the porch and counted stars, up in the sky and down by the fence, until Mira fell asleep.
