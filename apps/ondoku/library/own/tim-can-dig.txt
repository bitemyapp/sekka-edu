Tim can dig.
===
Tim digs and digs.
===
Kim has a big bag. Kim can dig, and Tim can fill the bag.
===
Dig, dig, dig! Tim hits a tin.
===
It is a big tin. What is in it?
===
Kim lifts the lid.
===
It is a map! The map has a pin.
===
Kim and Tim dig at the pin.
===
It is a tin of jam! Kim and Tim grin.
