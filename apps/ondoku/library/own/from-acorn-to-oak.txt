title: From Acorn to Oak
level: 3
skills: vocabulary, fluency
cover: leaf, #7b6a2f
about: How does a tiny acorn become a giant oak tree? Find out, step by step.

An oak tree can grow taller than a house and live for hundreds of years. But every oak begins as something small enough to fit in your hand: an acorn.
===
An acorn is a seed with a hard shell and a little cap. Inside the shell is a tiny plant, curled up and waiting, and a store of food to help it start.
===
In the fall, acorns drop to the ground. Squirrels and jays carry many of them away and bury them to eat in the winter. The ones they forget can sprout!
===
In the spring, the ground gets warm and wet. The acorn soaks up water, and its shell splits open. First, a root pushes down into the soil to drink.
===
Next, a shoot pushes up toward the light, and soon it unfolds its first leaves. Leaves are like tiny kitchens: they use sunlight, water and air to make food for the plant.
===
Year after year, the little oak grows taller. Its stem becomes a woody trunk, with bark to protect it, and its roots spread wide under the ground.
===
After twenty years or more, the oak makes acorns of its own. They drop to the ground in the fall, and the squirrels come again.
